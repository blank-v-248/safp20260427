I'm Luca Moretti — a Vienna-based photographer working at the intersection of portraiture, architecture, and landscape. My camera is a tool for slowing down, for finding the weight inside an ordinary scene.

I grew up in the Italian Alps, where light behaves like a living thing — golden at dawn, cruel at midday, consoling at dusk. That upbringing shaped everything I do behind the lens. I've carried that obsession with light across eighteen countries and into studios, forests, city streets, and private homes.

With over twelve years of professional work, I've collaborated with editorial clients across Europe, private collectors seeking something permanent, and couples who want their story told without clichés. Every session is a collaboration, every frame a shared decision.

I believe photography is not about capturing reality — it's about selecting which reality deserves to be remembered. The medium should disappear. Only the feeling should remain.

I shoot on both film and digital, choosing the format that best serves the mood. Slow, deliberate work on a Hasselblad for editorial and fine art; fast, responsive work on Sony for portraits and events.
