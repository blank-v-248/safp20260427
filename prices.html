FAQ

How far in advance should I book? +
Portrait and half-day sessions can often be booked within 1–2 weeks. Weddings should be booked 6–12 months in advance, as popular dates fill quickly. Commercial bookings depend on project scope.
How long until I receive my images? +
Portrait sessions are typically delivered within 2–3 weeks. Weddings take 4–6 weeks. Rush delivery (48h) is available as an add-on for portrait sessions.
Do you travel internationally? +
Yes. I'm available for travel anywhere in the world. Travel costs (flights, accommodation) are billed at cost, plus a daily travel fee of €200. Contact me for a custom quote.
What is your cancellation policy? +
A 30% deposit secures your date. Cancellations made more than 14 days before the session receive a full refund of the deposit. Cancellations within 14 days forfeit the deposit.
Do you shoot in bad weather? +
Weather is part of photography. Rain, fog, and overcast skies often produce the most interesting light. If conditions are truly unsafe, we'll reschedule at no charge.

Still have questions? I'm happy to chat before you commit.
